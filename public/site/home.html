Your pictures

A mirror, not a camera.

  • Nothing is recorded.

    The camera shows you to yourself. One frame leaves the mirror only when you ask for a look, and the mirror asks you first.

  • Store mirrors keep nothing.

    A portrait made in a store is shown, then gone, unless you send it to your own phone.

  • You choose who sees.

    Ask the family with your portraits or with the store’s photos. Links close by themselves.

  • You can delete everything.

    Your wardrobe, your portraits, your account. From your phone, in one step, without asking anyone.

Short answers

What people ask first.

Is it really free?
Yes. In a store you need no account at all. Joining is free and stays free: your kept looks on your phone, your sizes remembered, asking the family, and six portraits a month at home.
How close is the picture to the real thing?
Close, and honest about it. The colours, print and embroidery are taken from the store’s own photo, and your face is your own. It is a likeness, not a fitting: the store’s photo is always one tap away, and the size comes from the store’s own chart.
If the store pays for MIRVA, do I pay more for the clothes?
No. A piece costs what it costs in that store, with the mirror or without it. The store pays MIRVA a monthly fee, the way it pays for its lights and its tills. MIRVA takes nothing from you and sets no prices.
Where does my picture go?
When you ask for a look, one picture is sent to the try-on engine’s servers abroad, which make the look and send it back. The mirror asks before the first one, and a light on the glass shows when the camera is on. A store mirror keeps nothing. At home, a portrait you keep is saved to your own wardrobe, and you can delete it, or everything, in one step. The privacy notice has the detail.
