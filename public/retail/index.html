A visit, start to finish

About four minutes. No staff needed to begin.

  1. 01

    She arrives.

    The mirror is a mirror until she steps up. Your name is on it, not ours.

  2. 02

    It asks.

    The occasion, the mood, the budget. Three taps, or one sentence.

  3. 03

    It chooses.

    Three looks from your catalogue, with a reason for each and the sizes you have.

  4. 04

    She wears it.

    As a studio portrait in fifteen seconds, or live in the glass, turning to see it move.

  5. 05

    She keeps it.

    One scan sends the looks to her phone, with what goes with them: the trouser, the dupatta, the shoes.

The pilot

Thirty days. One store. A written result.

Before either of us signs for a year, we agree what success means, in numbers, and measure it in one of your stores.

Ask about a pilot
  1. Before day one.

    We load your catalogue, agree the measures, and install the mirror. Your staff get twenty minutes of training.

  2. Days 1 to 30.

    The mirror runs in the store. You see the numbers every day in your console; we review them with you every week.

  3. Day 30.

    A short written report against the measures we agreed. You decide whether to keep it. If not, we take the mirror away.

Your shoppers, your data

Built for a country where a woman's photograph matters.

  • Nothing is recorded.

    The camera is a mirror. A single frame leaves it only when she asks for a look, and a store mirror stores no portraits.

  • Her choice, every time.

    A notice at the mirror says what happens in plain words. Nothing reaches her phone unless she scans the code herself.

  • Your numbers stay yours.

    What happens in your stores is never used to sell for another retailer, and no other retailer sees it.

  • You hold the controls.

    Pair or unpair a mirror from your console, hide a piece, change how MIRVA dresses for you. No call to us needed.

Straight answers

What people ask us first.

How accurate is the picture?
It is a likeness, good enough to choose with. Colour, cut and drape come out well. Fine embroidery and small prints come out close, not exact. It helps a shopper decide what to try; it does not replace the fitting.
What do you need from us?
Your catalogue with one clear front picture of each piece, a spot near the entrance with a power socket, and a wired or strong Wi-Fi connection. We bring and install the mirror.
Does it connect to our till or stock system?
Not yet. Today MIRVA reads the sizes and stock on your website. For Boutique, a sale is matched by a code at the till. A direct connection is something we would build with a first chain partner.
What if the internet drops?
Portraits and live looks need a connection. If it drops, the mirror says so in plain words and carries on when the line is back. We test the store's line before we install anything.
Is it in Urdu?
The mirror speaks English today. Urdu is next, written by a native speaker rather than translated by a machine.
Who is behind MIRVA?
MIRVA is new and small, and built in Lahore. That is why the first stores get the founder's phone number and a say in what is built next.
